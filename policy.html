Code of Conduct & Operating Policy

Our commitment to transparency, integrity, and operational excellence.

A. Our Promise to Client

  • Transparency: No hiding charges; we only charge on applicable fees such as duty levy, VAT, shipping, and terminal charges.
  • Update: We give you daily updates on your cargo position.
  • Integrity: No under-declaration, no false classification, and no forgery.
  • Timely Delivery: We clear and deliver within a great timeframe, barring unforeseen custom or shipping delays, which we will communicate immediately.
  • Safety: We ensure the safe handling and delivery of your goods.

2. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not clear prohibited or contraband goods.
  • We do not engage in fake declarations to beat duty.
  • We do not abandon client cargo for demurrage to accumulate.
  • We do not inflate custom duty or terminal charges.
  • We do not use touts or unlicensed agents.

3. What We Expect From Our Client

  • Honest declaration of goods with correct invoicing and packing list.
  • Timely provision of Form M, PAAR, Bill of Lading, and other required documents.
  • No requests for illegal clearing methods.
  • Prompt payment of agreed charges to avoid demurrage/storage fees.
  • Respect and understanding that port operations have established procedures.
